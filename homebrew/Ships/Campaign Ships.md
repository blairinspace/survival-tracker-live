# Campaign Ships

> Homebrew profiles for the Through Darkness campaign, in the same layout as the vault's Starship Profiles. Fleet numbers follow the Rhigar Order of Battle. The X-wing, Y-wing, TIE/LN, Lambda shuttle and CR90 come straight from the Core Rulebook notes. Tune freely.

## The Crew

### The Mantis
Cal's ship: an S-161 "Stinger" XL luxury yacht, modified within an inch of its life. Flies under the transponder names Shyyyo, Longshot, Enterprise and Hiaboosa.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 6 | +0 | 2 / 2 / 2 / 0 | 4 | 25 | 15 |

**Hull Type/Class:** Yacht/S-161 Stinger XL · **Hyperdrive:** Primary: Class 1, Backup: yes · **Navicomputer:** Yes · **Sensor Range:** Short · **Complement:** One pilot (the chair is built for a four-armed Latero), co-pilot, gunners · **Encumbrance Capacity:** 100 · **Passenger Capacity:** 10 · **Consumables:** One month · **Hard Points:** 4

**Weapons:**
- Two forward heavy blaster cannons (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 5; Critical 4; Range [Close]; Linked 1)
- Proton torpedo launcher (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 8; Critical 2; Range [Short]; Blast 6, Breach 6, Guided 2, Limited Ammo 12, Slow-Firing 1)
- Rear laser cannon (Fire Arc Aft; Damage 9; Critical 3; Range [Extreme])
- Medium turbolaser (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 10; Critical 3; Range [Long]; Breach 3, Slow-Firing 1)

**Abilities:** Unseen (Merrin can hide the ship from sight and sensors at 4 strain per hour); Smuggling Compartments (10 encumbrance of hidden space); Live Tracking (fuel, system wear and energy routing are on the Ship tab, and its numbers win over these)

**Weapons:**
- Plasma projector (Fire Arc Forward; Damage 6; Critical 3; Range [Close]; Burn 2, Pierce 2)
