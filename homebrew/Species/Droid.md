# Droid

> **Published species** (Edge of the Empire), kept here because that book is not in the rules vault. Written in the same layout as the vault's species notes so the importer reads it. `droid: true` shows it only for the roster's droids; `career_free` / `spec_free` are how many free ranks a droid picks; `soak` is the rank of Enduring it starts with.

Droids are built, not born. A droid begins with every characteristic at 1 and a large pool of starting experience to shape itself with, so two droids of the same model can be very different machines.

## Species Abilities

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Brawn | 1 |
| Agility | 1 |
| Intellect | 1 |
| Cunning | 1 |
| Willpower | 1 |
| Presence | 1 |
| Wound Threshold | 10 + Brawn |
| Strain Threshold | 10 + Willpower |
| Starting XP | 175 XP |

- **Starting Skill Ranks:** A droid picks six of its career's skills, not four, for its free ranks, and three of its first specialization's skills, not two.
- **Inorganic:** Droids do not eat, sleep or breathe and are immune to poisons and toxins. They heal with Mechanics, repair patches and oil baths, never with Medicine, stimpacks or bacta.
- **Mechanical Being:** Droids cannot become Force sensitive, gain a Force rating or use Force powers, and cannot be affected by mind-altering Force powers.
- **Enduring:** A droid starts with one rank of the Enduring talent (+1 soak).
