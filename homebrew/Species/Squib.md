# Squib

> **Homebrew species** — not in a published FFG book. Same layout as the vault's species notes, so the importer reads it.

Squibs are small, furred, quick-talking traders and scavengers from Skor II. They stand about a metre tall, with large eyes, tufted ears and short muzzles. A Squib's fur is a sense organ: rubbing an object against it tells the Squib what the thing is made of and roughly what it is worth. They are compulsive hagglers and collectors who will pick up almost anything on the chance it can be traded, and they are cheerful, curious and all but fearless about wandering into places they should not be.
