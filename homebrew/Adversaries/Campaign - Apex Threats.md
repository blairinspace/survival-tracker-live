# Campaign: Apex Threats

> Homebrew, written for the Through Darkness campaign and a party near 1,200 XP. Tune freely.

### Darth Vader, Rebuilt [Nemesis]

The Emperor's enforcer, back from the living planet with new limbs and less patience. Built to stand against the whole crew at once.

- **Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Coercion 5, Cool 4, Discipline 5, Knowledge (Lore) 3, Lightsaber 6, Mechanics 3, Perception 4, Piloting (Space) 5, Resilience 4, Vigilance 4
- **Talents:** Adversary 5 (upgrade difficulty of all combat checks against this target five times); Parry 7 (when struck by a melee attack, suffer 3 strain to reduce damage by 9); Reflect 7 (when struck by a ranged attack, suffer 3 strain to reduce damage by 9); Improved Parry (when he parries a hit that generated [DE] or [TH][TH][TH], he may hit the attacker once with his lightsaber, dealing base damage); Durable 3 (subtract 30 from Critical Injury rolls against him)
- **Abilities:** Force Rating 6 (rolls six Force dice and may use dark side results freely); Relentless (acts twice each round: once in his own slot and again at the end of the round); Terrifying (at the start of the encounter each enemy makes a Daunting fear check with Discipline); Force Choke (action, one target within medium: opposed Discipline, on a success the target is immobilized and suffers 6 strain plus 1 per extra [SU] until the end of his next turn); Force Hurl (action: throw one silhouette 2 object or person at a target within long as a Discipline attack, Damage 20, Knockdown); Saber Throw (may attack with his lightsaber out to medium range and it returns to his hand); More Machine Than Man (immune to poison and vacuum for one hour. Ion weapons deal their damage to him as strain); Indomitable (once per session, when he would be incapacitated, he instead stays up with wounds equal to his threshold)
- **Equipment:** Lightsaber (Lightsaber; Damage 10; Critical 1; Range [Engaged]; Breach 1, Sunder, Vicious 2), armored life-support suit (+2 soak, +1 defense, sealed)

- **Abilities:** Force Rating 8 (rolls eight Force dice and may use any result freely); Relentless (acts twice each round: once in her own slot and again at the end of the round); Dread (at the start of the encounter each enemy makes a Formidable fear check with Discipline, and Force users add [SB] to it); Eternal (Force powers used directly against her add [SB][SB] and upgrade their difficulty twice. She returns later unless whatever let her reach out is closed)
