# Campaign: Imperial Forces

> Homebrew, written for the Through Darkness campaign and a party near 1,200 XP. Tune freely.

### Inquisitor [Nemesis]

Any Brother or Sister answering the summons to Bastion. Change the species, the saber style and one Force power to make each one different.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 |

**Soak** 5 · **Wound threshold** 22 · **Strain threshold** 20 · **Defense (melee / ranged)** 1 / 1

- **Skills:** Athletics 3, Coercion 3, Coordination 3, Discipline 3, Knowledge (Lore) 2, Lightsaber 4, Perception 3, Piloting (Space) 3, Stealth 2, Vigilance 3
- **Talents:** Adversary 3 (upgrade difficulty of all combat checks against this target three times); Parry 4 (when struck by a melee attack, suffer 3 strain to reduce damage by 6); Reflect 4 (when struck by a ranged attack, suffer 3 strain to reduce damage by 6)
- **Abilities:** Force Rating 3 (rolls three Force dice and may use dark side results freely); Force Hurl (action: throw one silhouette 1 object at a target within medium as a Discipline attack, Damage 10, Knockdown); Force Bind (action, one target within short: opposed Discipline, on a success the target is immobilized until the end of the Inquisitor's next turn); Saber Throw (may attack with the lightsaber out to medium range and it returns to hand); Spinning Flight (maneuver: the spinning hilt carries the Inquisitor to any point within short range, including straight up or across a gap)
- **Equipment:** Double-bladed spinning lightsaber (Lightsaber; Damage 7; Critical 2; Range [Engaged]; Breach 1, Linked 1, Sunder, Unwieldy 3), armored uniform (+2 soak)
