# Campaign: Centerpoint and the Maw

> Homebrew, written for the Through Darkness campaign and a party near 1,200 XP. Tune freely.

### Centerpoint Security Trooper [Minion]

Station security at the thousand or so internal checkpoints between the docks and the control room.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |

**Soak** 4 · **Wound threshold** 5 · **Defense (melee / ranged)** 0 / 0

- **Skills (group only):** Melee, Perception, Ranged (Heavy), Vigilance
- **Talents:** None
- **Abilities:** Checkpoint (at a checkpoint terminal, may raise the station alarm as a maneuver. Scanners add [SB][SB] to checks using forged credentials below top level); Station Hand (ignores penalties from low or shifting gravity)
- **Equipment:** Blaster carbine (Ranged [Heavy]; Damage 9; Critical 3; Range [Medium]; Stun setting), stun baton (Melee; Damage +2; Critical 5; Range [Engaged]; Disorient 2, Stun Damage), sealed duty armor (+1 soak), comlink

### Thuruht Drone [Minion]

Eight-limbed insectoid builders of the hive that raised Centerpoint beside the Celestials. A few dozen were left to tend it.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 3 | 3 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 |

**Soak** 5 · **Wound threshold** 6 · **Defense (melee / ranged)** 0 / 0

- **Skills (group only):** Athletics, Brawl, Coordination, Mechanics, Perception
- **Talents:** None
- **Abilities:** Hive Mind (what one drone knows, all know. They are never surprised if any one of them is aware, and are immune to fear); Many Limbs (adds [BO] to Brawl checks and may climb any surface as normal movement); Builders (a group repairs or seals a door, conduit or bulkhead in one round)
- **Equipment:** Mandibles and claws (Brawl; Damage +2; Critical 3; Range [Engaged]; Pierce 1)

Force heretics living in the Maw who hold that life, not death, is the illusion.

|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 |

- **Talents:** Adversary 2 (upgrade difficulty of all combat checks against this target twice)
