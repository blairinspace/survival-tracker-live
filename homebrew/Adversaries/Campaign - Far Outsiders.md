# Campaign: Far Outsiders

> Homebrew, written for the Through Darkness campaign and a party near 1,200 XP. Tune freely. Every Yuuzhan Vong and every creature they grow is absent from the Force.

### Yuuzhan Vong Warrior [Rival]

The warrior caste: scarred, armored in living shell, and glad of pain.

| Brawn | Agility | Intellect | Cunning | Willpower | Presence |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 2 |

**Soak** 7 · **Wound threshold** 18 · **Defense (melee / ranged)** 1 / 1

- **Skills:** Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Coercion 3, Discipline 3, Melee 4, Perception 2, Ranged (Light) 2, Resilience 3, Vigilance 2
- **Talents:** Adversary 2 (upgrade difficulty of all combat checks against this target twice); Parry 2 (when struck by a melee attack, the warrior reduces damage by 4)
- **Abilities:** Outside the Force (cannot be sensed through the Force or targeted directly by Force powers. Objects moved by the Force can still hit them); Pain Is Devotion (immune to fear and to Coercion by pain. After suffering a Critical Injury the warrior adds [BO] to their next check); Vonduun Crab Armor (Breach used against the warrior counts as Pierce 5 instead); Resonance Weakness (sound at 6,200 or 37,000 Hz disorients the warrior and makes living gear add [SB][SB] to its checks)
- **Equipment:** Amphistaff (Melee; Damage +4; Critical 2; Range [Engaged]; Cortosis, Defensive 1, Pierce 2, Vicious 2), thud bugs (Ranged [Light]; Damage 7; Critical 4; Range [Short]; Disorient 3, Knockdown, Limited Ammo 3), razor bugs (Ranged [Light]; Damage 5; Critical 2; Range [Medium]; Limited Ammo 3, Pierce 2, Vicious 2), vonduun crab armor (+3 soak, +1 defense)
